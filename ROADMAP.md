# Roadmap

Ideas, roughly in the order they'll be looked at. Nothing here is promised;
suggestions are welcome as [issues](https://github.com/TTV-VibesByVince/Govee-Lights-Controller/issues/new/choose).

## Curtain Lights
- **Text on the curtain:** scroll a short message or a follower's name across
  the 30×32 grid, drawn by the app (no GIF needed).
- **GIFs on alert presets:** one `/alert/raid` that runs a room effect *and* a GIF.
- **Animated entrances:** profile pictures that slide in or sit in a coloured frame.
- **Built-in curtain effects:** generated rainbow, fire and countdown animations.
- **Idle playlist:** rotate saved GIFs every few minutes.
- **Crop choice:** fit the whole picture with black bars instead of centre-cropping.

## Streaming
- **Scenes that include a curtain GIF.**
- **Stream-state presets:** one URL for "starting", "BRB" and "ending".
- **Friendlier replies for chat:** "queued, 2 ahead of you", "cooldown, 6s left".
- **Blocklist for chat GIFs.**

## The app
- **Working command cooldown** for room commands.
- **Strobe safety limit:** cap the flash rate and length for viewer-triggered strobes.
- **Brightness by number:** `/brightness/40`.
- **Light groups:** e.g. "desk" and "wall", each with its own URLs.
- **Start minimised and launch with Windows.**
- **Activity log saved to a file.**
- **The project icon as the app's window and taskbar icon.**
- **Mac and Linux:** check and fix what's Windows-only.
